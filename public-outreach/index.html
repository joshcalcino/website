PUBLIC OUTREACH

Science is my passion, and I love sharing that passion. I want to make others as excited as I am about just how much we know, and how much more we can learn as a species. I have given half a dozen hour long public lectures, visited schools in cities and rural areas, written articles for The Conversation, and filmed a short segment for the kids TV show Scope.

Here are some comments I have received from my public lectures:

"So Amazing! Really appreciated the simple to understand language, well structured, so informative, great energy and enthusiasm."

“Impeccable presentation. Blown away. Thank you.”

"Very interesting, informative and very well presented."

"Very informative, great presenter, made subject interesting and understandable."
